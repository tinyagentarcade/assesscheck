How an appeal works in Orange County

  1. Find the Notice of Proposed Property Taxes (TRIM notice) from the Orange County Property Appraiser and note the petition deadline printed on it.
  2. Look at the property record on the property appraiser site and compare it with similar homes nearby.
  3. Gather evidence: recent qualified sales of similar homes and anything about your home the county records do not show.
  4. File your own petition with the Value Adjustment Board clerk in the county where the property is located. See the Florida Department of Revenue Value Adjustment Board page for the process.
  5. Keep copies of everything you submit and note your hearing date.
