    How a property tax appeal works

    1. Look at your notice of appraised value and your record on your county appraisal district or property appraiser site.
    2. Gather evidence: similar homes appraised lower, and recent sales if you have them.
    3. File your own protest (Texas: appraisal district) or petition (Florida: value adjustment board) by the deadline on your notice. See HCAD's protest page DCAD's site or Florida DOR's value adjustment board page for forms, hearing rules and dates.
    4. Keep copies of everything and note your hearing date.

    Deadlines are set by the county and by your notice. Confirm them on your county's official page before you act. We do not file anything for you.
