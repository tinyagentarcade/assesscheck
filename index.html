    How a Harris County protest works

    1. Look at your notice of appraised value and your record on the Harris Central Appraisal District (HCAD) site.
    2. Gather evidence: similar homes appraised lower, and recent sales if you have them.
    3. File your own protest with HCAD by the deadline on your notice. See HCAD's protest page for forms, hearing rules and dates.
    4. Keep copies of everything and note your hearing date.

    Deadlines are set by the county and by your notice. Confirm them on HCAD's page before you act. We do not file anything for you.
