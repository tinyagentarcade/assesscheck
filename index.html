    How a Texas property tax protest works

    1. Look at your notice of appraised value and your record on your appraisal district's site (HCAD for Harris, DCAD for Dallas).
    2. Gather evidence: similar homes appraised lower, and recent sales if you have them.
    3. File your own protest with your appraisal district by the deadline on your notice. See HCAD's protest page or DCAD's site for forms, hearing rules and dates.
    4. Keep copies of everything and note your hearing date.

    Deadlines are set by the county and by your notice. Confirm them on your appraisal district's page before you act. We do not file anything for you.
