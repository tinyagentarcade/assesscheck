How an appeal works in Dallas County

  1. Find your notice of appraised value from Dallas Central Appraisal District (DCAD) and note the protest deadline printed on it.
  2. Look at your online record and compare it with similar homes in your neighborhood.
  3. Gather evidence: homes similar to yours that are appraised lower, and anything about your home the county records do not show.
  4. File your own protest with the appraisal district by the deadline. See the DCAD website for forms and hearing rules.
  5. Keep copies of everything you submit and note your hearing date.
